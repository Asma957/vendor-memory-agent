# Social post (LinkedIn)

Most AI memory demos are about remembering more. In procurement, the bigger risk is remembering something that is no longer true.

At Hack With Hyderabad 3.0, our team [TEAM NAMES] built the **Vendor Memory Agent** on Hindsight by Vectorize.

It remembers how each vendor negotiates: who says no first, who is firm, who gives discounts at quarter end. And when a new contract arrives, it marks the old terms as outdated and tells the rep what replaced them, so nobody walks into a meeting quoting last year's pricing.

The app compares a Baseline agent with our Smart agent on the same Hindsight memory, so you can see the difference forgetting makes.
